A talk I gave at Spark Summit East 2017 with my mentor Robbie Strickland.

Parquet is a big data storage format that wag integral to our analytics workflow.
This talk details the ins and outs of the format in connection with Apache Spark.

Systems like BigQuery are essentially Parquet under the hood, so understanding the details and tradeoffs of this format can be really helpful for reasoning through the performance aspects of large systems.

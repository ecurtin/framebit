# Career Stuff

I'm Emily from MLE, partly because the branding is too good to get away from it.

This one time I went to film school _so I'm secretly cool_ but these days I'm a Staff ML Engineer at Intuit Mailchimp.

I lead a squad that partners with Data Scientists, Prompt Engineers, AI Engineers, Product Engineers, and Data Engineers (the most important, btw) to get all kinds of stuff to prod. We focus on scalability, cloud stuff, the blurry edges between ML and Data Eng, DevEx for Data Science, and operational excellence in production. Sometimes we save million$ of dollar$ along the way, which is pretty cool.

Some would say I'm more MLOps than MLEng, but impact matters a bit more than definitions in this space at this time.

One of my favorite things about my job is getting the opportunity to speak and put a human spin on these machine problems. I also enjoy mentoring early career engineers and watching the lightbulbs turn on as concepts connect.
